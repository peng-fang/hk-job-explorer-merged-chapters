## 0:00–0:50 · Opening

[首页，语言 English。]

Meet Jia Ning. She has just completed her master’s degree in Hong Kong and is considering staying here to work and build a life. She has a qualification and some interests, but she still has practical questions. Where should she look? How should she interpret the market? And what would make a job financially and personally workable?

Our project is an explorer built around that process. We will follow Jia Ning through four steps, watch her use the charts, and change one of her assumptions. By the end, she will have a shortlist of directions and a clearer set of questions to take into her job search.

## 0:50–2:25 · 00 Two markets

[滚到 Employed population by age 和 Workers aged 35 and over by industry。]

Jia Ning begins with a broad comparison between Mainland China and Hong Kong.

The first chart uses mirrored bars. Each row represents the same age group, with one market on each side. This makes the age profiles easy to compare without asking the audience to remember two separate charts.

The second chart compares the share of workers aged thirty-five and over within each industry. The two bars in a row refer to the same broad industry. Jia Ning can also switch between the earlier and more recent snapshots to see whether the pattern changes.

These charts give her leads to investigate. For example, she can ask whether an industry that interests her has a different workforce structure in Hong Kong. An older workforce alone does not tell us whether employers prefer older applicants. The chart describes people already working in the industry.

The dates and definitions also matter. The industry snapshots are one year apart across the two markets, and some Mainland breakdowns come from project estimates. We therefore use this comparison to frame questions for the next step.

Jia Ning now wants to translate these broad industry interests into possible jobs in Hong Kong.

## 2:25–4:20 · 01 Employment landscape

[进入 01。指向总量折线和行业面积图。]

The employment landscape helps Jia Ning move from a market to a search direction.

The line chart shows total employment over time. Beside it, the stacked area chart shows how employment is distributed across industries. We read them together because an industry’s share can increase even while total employment falls.

[Linked year 选 2019，再选 2025。向下展示环形图和树图。]

When I change the selected year, the employment status and occupation views update. The donut chart shows employees, employers, the self-employed and other arrangements. Jia Ning initially focuses on employee roles.

The treemap then gives her a vocabulary for searching. The size of each rectangle represents the number of people in an occupation. She can explore professional, associate professional, clerical, or other roles and read their full labels.

This distinction matters. An industry describes what an employer does, while an occupation describes the work a person performs. A graduate searching only for an industry name may miss relevant occupations in other industries.

Jia Ning now has a few directions to investigate. Before applying, she asks two connected questions. What pressure might people around her age face? And how much can industry earnings really tell her about a potential role?

## 4:20–7:10 · 02 Risk and pay context

[进入 02。风险年和薪酬年保持 2025。]

We bring those two questions together in this chapter.

On the left, the heatmap compares unemployment across ages and years. Darker cells indicate higher rates, and every cell also carries a number. Selecting a cell chooses both an age group and a year.

[点 Age 25–29 的 2025 格，再点 Age 20–24 的 2025 格。]

In the source data, the unemployment rate in 2025 is 5.2 percent for ages twenty-five to twenty-nine, compared with 11.2 percent for ages twenty to twenty-four. This shows why Jia Ning should look beyond one citywide figure when thinking about the market.

She uses that context to consider parallel applications and some financial buffer. These are group rates. The time she personally needs to find a job also depends on her experience, skills, applications and the roles available.

On the right, paired bars compare women’s and men’s median monthly earnings within each industry. Putting the bars on a common scale makes the differences easy to inspect.

For Jia Ning, the useful response is to ask more specific questions. What is the grade of this role? What is its salary band? What are the actual hours and promotion arrangements? Occupation, experience, working hours and the share of full-time workers can all affect group medians. These aggregates cannot by themselves establish unequal pay for the same work.

[指向两个折叠区域，主展示不展开。]

The explorer also lets her investigate further. One expandable section covers quarterly unemployment, past downturns and education groups. The other covers earnings trends and labour force participation. Each set retains its own reference year, and switching language preserves the selected values.

Jia Ning now has a more informed question. Given the pressure she may face and the terms she needs to verify, which options could work with her own pay, time and living-cost assumptions?

## 7:10–12:50 · 03 Explore job options

[进入 03，点击 Load Jia Ning’s example。]

This final chapter brings the exploration into a personal scenario. I will load Jia Ning’s example so that we can reproduce the same choices.

She is initially looking at four broad areas, including finance, professional and business services, accommodation and food services, and real estate. She sets a monthly pay target of twenty-five thousand Hong Kong dollars, a weekly hours limit of forty-two, and a target monthly surplus of six thousand.

[展示 Hiring opportunities。]

The hiring map gives her a first view of demand. The horizontal position is the vacancy rate. The vertical position is employment growth compared with the same quarter a year earlier. Bubble area shows the number of vacancies.

The horizontal reference line marks zero employment growth, while the vertical line marks the survey-wide vacancy rate. A bubble can therefore have a relatively high vacancy rate even when employment is declining. Looking at the two dimensions together gives more context than sorting industries by one number.

Selecting an industry updates the occupation vacancies, pay ranges, hours ranges, history and shortlist. The vacancy heatmap helps Jia Ning ask what kinds of work are being advertised within that industry. She can switch from counts to shares to compare its occupational mix.

[向下到 Monthly pay range 和 Weekly hours range。]

Next, she examines the terms of work. The pale bands show the twenty-fifth to seventy-fifth percentiles, the middle half of the distribution. The dot shows the median, and the reference line shows her chosen target or limit.

This is useful because an industry median hides a range of experiences. Even when a median looks acceptable, the hours range may give her a reason to ask about overtime during an interview.

[展示 Monthly budget。]

The waterfall chart turns an assumed offer into a monthly living scenario. Jia Ning has entered ten thousand dollars for rent, six thousand five hundred for living and transport, and two thousand as an allowance for deductions. With an offer of twenty-five thousand, the estimated monthly surplus is six thousand five hundred.

These inputs are editable. She can replace them with her actual rent or offer later. The deductions allowance also needs to reflect her circumstances, including applicable tax and MPF arrangements.

[向下到 Shortlist comparison，必要时简单指出历史折线。]

The shortlist now checks three criteria together. Median pay must reach her target. Median weekly hours must stay within her limit. And the estimated surplus, using each industry’s median pay, must meet her savings target.

Under the example’s forty-two-hour limit, finance and insurance, and professional, scientific and technical activities meet all three conditions. Other rows explain which condition is not met. The table therefore gives her a practical reason to investigate some directions first.

[回到 Weekly hours limit，42 改 44，点击 Apply criteria，再看候选表。]

Now Jia Ning changes one assumption. She is willing to consider up to forty-four hours a week. Real estate activities now join the options that meet her criteria.

This is the central interaction in our story. A small change in a personal constraint changes what she may investigate. She can decide whether that trade-off is acceptable, then return to the actual job descriptions and ask about their working arrangements.

[可略。点专业科学及技术服务的月薪圆点，展示预算变化。]

She can also click a salary median to use it as the assumed offer. Using twenty-nine thousand five hundred gives an estimated surplus of eleven thousand with the same expenses. That is a scenario to test against a real offer.

Demand here refers to broad industries in the second quarter of 2026. The pay and hours figures refer to full-time employees in the selected pay year. We keep those periods visible so that the comparison remains interpretable.

## 12:50–14:00 · Closing

[停留 Shortlist comparison。]

Jia Ning finishes with a more focused plan. She has moved from comparing markets, to identifying occupations, to understanding risk and earnings, and finally to testing her own constraints.

Her shortlist is conditional. If she keeps the forty-two-hour limit, she investigates the first set of directions. If she accepts forty-four hours, another option enters. As she receives job descriptions or actual offers, she can update her assumptions and explore again.

That is the role of this explorer. It connects statistical evidence with the questions a graduate needs to ask, while keeping the next action visible. For Jia Ning, the next action is to check specific roles, pay, hours, work eligibility and conditions, then make a decision using the information she has verified.

Thank you.

## 现场追问备忘

**为什么合并原 02 与 03？**

它们共同回答「找到岗位方向之后、投递之前还要查什么」。年龄失业率解释求职压力，行业男女薪酬提醒读者深入核实岗位条件。随后进入个人试算，前后衔接更自然。

**为什么延伸图折叠？**

主展示聚焦两个问题，想深查时仍能展开历史和教育资料。图表、数据与原有筛选联动保留。

**能估计多久找到工作吗？**

现有群体失业率不能估计个人求职时长。本版把问题改成识别压力、安排缓冲和并行投递。

**为何男女中位数不同？**

这组数据展示群体差异，职业、年资、工时等构成可能参与解释。若要判断相同工作中的薪酬差异，需要可比岗位层面的资料。

**为什么点行业后预算不一定改变？**

行业选择更新行业视图与候选表。个人预算使用输入的 Offer。点击工资 P50 圆点会将该值带入 Offer，预算才随之更新。

**哪些数据最容易被误读？**

00 的行业快照存在年份和估计限制。02 的失业与收入来自不同群体口径。03 的空缺为行业大类，薪酬为细分行业，且调查期不同。图下小字和来源页保留这些说明。
